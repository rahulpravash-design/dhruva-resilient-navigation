# DHRUVA results

Generated by `python -m dhruva.eval --config configs/eval_quick.yaml`. Do not edit by hand. Every number below is computed from `runs/eval_quick/cases.jsonl`.

> **All numbers on this page are SYNTHETIC.** They come from a simulator with an *assumed* phone IMU/GNSS model and an *assumed* speed-dependent vibration model (`docs/SPEEDNET_ASSUMPTIONS.md`). They show how the engine behaves in that model. They are not evidence of real-world accuracy.

## 6. How to read this

- Ablations are cumulative: each row adds one component to the row above.
- Re-entry jump is the largest displayed step beyond expected motion (speed x dt) in the 3 s after GNSS returns.
- Coverage is the share of outage epochs whose true error is inside the filter's own 95 % ellipse radius (target about 95 %). The baseline has no covariance, so it shows —.
- Known limits are listed in `docs/BLOCKERS.md`; the integrity thresholds were tuned on the `val` split only.
